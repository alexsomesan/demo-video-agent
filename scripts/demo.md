# Show workspaces in Explorer

## Before recording

* Open `app.terraform.io` and login with the credentials from _creds.md_
* Wait for the "Organizations" page to fully load.

## Begin recording

* Select the "ILM_Demo_Space" organization.
* From the left-hand side-bar select "Explorer Graph"
* From the "Browse" drop-down, select "Workspaces without VCS"
* Toggle the view mode to "Graph"
* From the graph, click to select the "huge-memory-workspace" node.
* Click the "+" button in the bottom right corner twice to zoom in.
* Drag the graph canvas to bring the "huge-memory-workspace" node closer to the center of the screen.
* Hide the left-hand side navigation bar by clicking on the minimizer tab at the top.
